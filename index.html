Peace, stability and prosperity are interconnected — the Centre exists to strengthen regional partnerships and advance shared development.

The Horn of Africa Centre for Regional Integration, founded and led by Abdurahman Sayed, promotes lasting peace and sustainable development through good governance, regional cooperation and deeper integration among the countries of the Horn of Africa. The Centre serves as a platform for dialogue, research, policy development and collaborative action.

Together, these four countries represent a region of immense strategic and economic significance — bound by deep geographical, social and historical ties that provide a strong foundation for cooperation.
